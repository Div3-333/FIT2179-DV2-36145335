  <div style="display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap; gap: 20px; position: relative; z-index: 10;">

    <div style="flex: 1 1 100%; background-color: #e0f2fe; border: 4px solid #0284c7; border-radius: 15px 225px 15px 255px/255px 15px 225px 15px; padding: 25px; box-shadow: 8px 8px 0px #0284c7; font-size: 17px; line-height: 1.8; background-image: radial-gradient(#7dd3fc 10%, transparent 11%); background-size: 15px 15px;">
      <div style="background: rgba(255,255,255,0.9); padding: 20px; border-radius: 10px; border: 3px dashed #0284c7;">
        <span style="font-weight: bold; font-size: 20px; color: #0f172a;">Domain and audience:</span> <span style="font-style: italic; font-weight: bold; text-decoration: underline; color: #0284c7;">From Sky to Tap</span> examines Victorian <span style="color: #0369a1; font-weight: bold; font-size: 19px;">water security 💧</span> under compounding <span style="color: #dc2626; font-weight: bold; font-size: 19px;">climate stress 🔥</span>: <span style="color: #b45309;">rainfall deficits 🍂</span>, <span style="color: #ef4444; text-transform: uppercase;">warming</span>, <span style="color: #ea580c; font-style: italic;">evaporation ♨️</span>, reservoir response, <span style="color: #16a34a; font-weight: bold;">groundwater pressure</span> and <span style="color: #7e22ce; font-weight: bold;">desalination ⚙️</span>. It is designed for a general Australian audience, not hydrologists: the goal is to make the system understandable to residents who know <span style="color: #b45309; text-decoration: underline;">drought</span> and <span style="color: #0284c7; text-decoration: underline;">water restrictions</span> but may not understand how <span style="background-color: #fca5a5; padding: 2px 5px; border-radius: 4px; border: 1px solid #000;">meteorological stress</span> becomes <span style="background-color: #cbd5e1; padding: 2px 5px; border-radius: 4px; border: 1px solid #000;">infrastructure stress</span>.
      </div>
    </div>

    <div style="flex: 1 1 45%; min-width: 300px; background-color: #fee2e2; border: 4px solid #b91c1c; border-radius: 255px 15px 225px 15px/15px 225px 15px 255px; padding: 25px; box-shadow: 8px 8px 0px #b91c1c; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8;">
      <span style="font-weight: bold; font-size: 20px; color: #b91c1c;">Why: 🧐</span> The visualisation addresses a public communication problem. Climate and water data are usually published as separate technical datasets, which makes it difficult to see the sequence from <span style="color: #ea580c; font-weight: bold;">weather anomaly 🌪️</span> to <span style="color: #0369a1; font-weight: bold;">supply response 🚰</span>. The project therefore asks: where is <span style="color: #92400e; font-weight: bold;">rainfall stress</span> concentrated, how has <span style="color: #ef4444; font-size: 22px; font-weight: bold; text-shadow: 1px 1px 0px #000;">heat 🌡️</span> shifted through time, how do climate drivers and <span style="color: #d97706; font-style: italic;">evaporation</span> intensify catchment pressure, and when does <span style="color: #7e22ce; font-weight: bold; font-family: monospace; font-size: 18px;">engineered supply 🏭</span> become significant? The story argues that Victoria’s water security is still fundamentally <span style="color: #2563eb; font-weight: bold; font-size: 18px;">rain-dependent 🌧️</span>, but <span style="color: #8b5cf6; font-weight: bold;">desalination</span> can operate as a <span style="background-color: #bbf7d0; padding: 2px 6px; border: 2px solid #16a34a; border-radius: 8px; transform: rotate(-2deg); display: inline-block;">stabilising buffer 🛡️</span> during low-storage periods.
    </div>

    <div style="flex: 1 1 45%; min-width: 300px; background-color: #dcfce7; border: 4px solid #15803d; border-radius: 15px 225px 15px 255px/255px 15px 225px 15px; padding: 25px; box-shadow: 8px 8px 0px #15803d; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8;">
      <span style="font-weight: bold; font-size: 20px; color: #15803d;">What: 📊</span> The data combines multiple reliable public sources: Bureau of Meteorology <span style="color: #2563eb; font-weight: bold;">rainfall 💧</span>, ACORN-SAT <span style="color: #ef4444; font-weight: bold;">temperature 🌡️</span> and Southern Oscillation Index records; SILO <span style="color: #ea580c; font-weight: bold;">evaporation ♨️</span>, rainfall and temperature data; Melbourne Water <span style="color: #0369a1; font-weight: bold;">reservoir storage 🌊</span>; Victorian reservoir capacity/location data; <span style="color: #16a34a; font-weight: bold;">groundwater 🌱</span> bore-permit density; Victorian Water Accounts resource-use data; and <span style="color: #7e22ce; font-weight: bold;">desalination/demand ⚙️</span> records from Victorian water planning documents. Derived data were used only to clarify structure: <span style="font-family: monospace; background: #fff; padding: 0 4px; border: 1px solid #ccc;">monthly rainfall anomalies</span>, two historical temperature windows, an OLS evaporation trendline, normalised water-resource shares, desalination dependency ratios, spatial interpolation/grid density, and storage-desalination state positions.
    </div>

    <div style="flex: 1 1 45%; min-width: 300px; background-color: #ffedd5; border: 4px solid #c2410c; border-radius: 225px 15px 255px 15px/15px 255px 15px 225px; padding: 25px; box-shadow: 8px 8px 0px #c2410c; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8;">
      <span style="font-weight: bold; font-size: 20px; color: #c2410c;">Why: 🧩</span> users are guided to <span style="background: #fff; border: 2px dashed #000; padding: 2px 6px; font-weight: bold;">discover 🔎</span> spatial patterns, <span style="background: #fff; border: 2px dashed #000; padding: 2px 6px; font-weight: bold;">compare ⚖️</span> historical and modern periods, <span style="background: #fff; border: 2px dashed #000; padding: 2px 6px; font-weight: bold;">identify 📌</span> temporal trends and anomalies, <span style="background: #fff; border: 2px dashed #000; padding: 2px 6px; font-weight: bold;">relate 🔗</span> climate signals to local rainfall, compare reservoir magnitude against percentage fullness, and <span style="background: #fff; border: 2px dashed #000; padding: 2px 6px; font-weight: bold;">summarise 📝</span> system state through final engineered-supply views. The intended task is not expert exploration; it is <span style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-style: wavy; text-decoration-color: #c2410c; font-weight: bold; font-size: 18px;">guided presentation</span> with enough interaction to test the narrative.
    </div>

    <div style="flex: 1 1 45%; min-width: 300px; background-color: #f3e8ff; border: 4px solid #7e22ce; border-radius: 15px 255px 15px 225px/225px 15px 255px 15px; padding: 25px; box-shadow: 8px 8px 0px #7e22ce; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8;">
      <span style="font-weight: bold; font-size: 20px; color: #7e22ce;">How: 🛠️</span> The design uses <span style="background-color: #1e293b; color: #fff; font-family: monospace; padding: 3px 6px; border-radius: 4px;">Vega-Lite</span> maps and diagrams embedded in a single scrolling narrative. Spatial marks locate rainfall deficit, reservoir capacity and groundwater stress. Temporal heatmaps, line charts, area charts and bar charts expose change over time. <span style="font-weight: bold; color: #d946ef; font-size: 18px;">Position 📍</span> encodes ordered quantitative values; <span style="font-weight: bold; color: #06b6d4; font-size: 18px; text-shadow: 1px 1px 0 #fff;">colour 🎨</span> is used sparingly for meaningful categories or intensity; <span style="font-weight: bold; color: #eab308; font-size: 22px;">size 📏</span> encodes reservoir capacity and rainfall magnitude where comparison benefits from magnitude cues. Annotations, arrows, tooltips and colour-coded textual terms connect visual evidence to prose without forcing the viewer to infer everything unaided. The page follows a logical Munzner What/Why/How progression: <span style="color: #0284c7; font-weight: bold;">sky/climate drivers</span>, <span style="color: #16a34a; font-weight: bold;">catchment response</span>, <span style="color: #b45309; font-weight: bold;">hidden groundwater buffer</span>, then <span style="color: #7e22ce; font-weight: bold;">engineered survival</span>.
    </div>

    <div style="flex: 1 1 100%; background-color: #fef08a; border: 4px solid #ca8a04; border-radius: 255px 15px 225px 15px/15px 225px 15px 255px; padding: 25px; box-shadow: 8px 8px 0px #ca8a04; font-size: 17px; line-height: 1.8; margin-top: 10px; transform: rotate(-0.5deg);">
      <span style="font-weight: bold; font-size: 20px;">Special features include ⭐</span> linked toggle states across the final three charts, normalised proportional composition, a bivariate survival matrix, and a methodology/audit section that documents sources, transformations and idiom rationale. Overall, the project transforms fragmented hydrological and climate records into a <span style="background-color: #fff; border: 3px solid #000; padding: 4px 8px; font-weight: bold; font-size: 18px; box-shadow: 3px 3px 0 #000; border-radius: 10px;">coherent explanatory story 📖</span> about Victoria’s transition from <span style="text-decoration: line-through; color: #64748b; font-weight: bold;">rainfall reliance 🌧️</span> toward <span style="color: #059669; font-weight: bold; font-size: 20px;">conditional engineered resilience 🏗️💧</span>.
    </div>
